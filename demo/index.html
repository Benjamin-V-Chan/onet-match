onet-match

Map free text about work to O*NET occupations: job titles, resume bullets, job postings, interests or skill lists.

Runs entirely in your browser; your text never leaves your device. A fine-tuned bge-base bi-encoder plus a cross-encoder reranker over all 923 O*NET 31.0 occupations, compressed to int8 for the browser (results can differ slightly from the Python package, which is the reference). The first pass shows instantly; the reranker then refines it. Suggestions only; not for hiring or eligibility decisions.
